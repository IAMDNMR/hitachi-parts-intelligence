## Europe scope rule for the supplier master (instruction §3)

`DQ-05`/`non_european_records` intentionally only checks `DealerBranch`/`Depot` and non-synthetic
suppliers that are NOT from `HCME_SAP_SUPPLIER_MASTER` — the 67 real suppliers whose country is
outside Europe (e.g. US, Japan, China — HCME's own vendor list, not a customer-facing operational
record) are retained, tagged `region: 'Rest of world'`, and never surface in a European
supplier-risk or dealer-context answer: `part_dealers`/`supplier_lookup`/`co_located` intelligence
handlers only ever traverse `Part→Assembly→Supplier` (demo suppliers only, all European) or
`DealerBranch/Depot→City` (always a real HCME Europe location), never the raw un-filtered Supplier
list. This is the exact rule instruction §3 asked to be documented.
