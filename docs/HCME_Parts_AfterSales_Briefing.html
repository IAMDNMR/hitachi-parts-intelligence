A Connected Parts-to-Service Journey

01 · DEALER AS PARTNER

Make the dealer a connected, first-class part of the journey — not just a sales point.

02 · VISIBILITY, END TO END

Know where stock exists, then how it reaches the customer — availability and fulfilment shown clearly, at every step.

03 · BUILD ON WHAT EXISTS

Ground the journey in Hitachi's real tools — ConSite, e-Service, Dealer Portal — not a public parts API that doesn't exist yet.

04 · ONE CONNECTED FLOW

Join order, shipment, dealer hand-off and service into one journey, instead of separate, disconnected steps.

05 · READY FOR REAL SYSTEMS

Design so real ERP, WMS, logistics and carrier systems can be connected progressively — without redesigning the core journey.
